Why feedbacks matter

Complex feedbacks in Earth's climate system, triggered by ongoing CO2 emissions and warming, can influence global warming potentially much further. Their impact over the coming decades is expected to be relatively minor, but they will determine the future of Earth's climate far beyond 2100.
