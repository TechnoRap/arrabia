Earth System Feedback Research Centre

Climate change now and beyond 2100

How will feedbacks in oceans, ice, land and atmosphere shape Earth's climate far beyond this century? EMBRACER brings together Earth scientists, oceanographers, polar researchers, hydrologists and ecologists to find out.

One system

Earth, water, ice, life and climate move together

More heatwaves in oceans, intensifying droughts on continents, thawing permafrost, melting icecaps. Consequences of climate change have begun emerging worldwide in the oceans, ice, land and atmosphere. Each change feeds back into the others.

Ice

Ice sheets, sea ice, glaciers

Bright ice reflects sunlight. As it melts, darker water absorbs more heat.

Water

Ocean, groundwater, rivers

Warming waters store less carbon and shift rain, snow and drought.

Life

Forests, algae, people

Fragile carbon sinks that can turn into sources.

Climate

Atmosphere

A warmer atmosphere holds more moisture and traps more heat.

Emerging climate feedbacks

Small for decades, decisive for centuries

Complex feedbacks in Earth's climate system, triggered by ongoing CO2 emissions and warming, can influence global warming potentially much further. While their impacts are expected to be relatively minor over the coming years to decades, they will determine the future of Earth's climate far beyond 2100.

Even in today's best climate models, scientific understanding of these feedbacks remains highly uncertain. This uncertainty risks underestimating sea level rise, the extremity of disruptive heatwaves, or spending huge resources on ineffective climate action plans.

Bridging the gap

From decades to millennia

EMBRACER connects different Earth-system components, timescales and methods. Earth scientists, geochemists, oceanographers, polar researchers, hydrologists and ecologists collaborate to bridge the knowledge gap between predicting short-term and long-term climate change.

The programme also enables junior researchers' professional and leadership development, and fosters long-term excellence and diversity in the field.
