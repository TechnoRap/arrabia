About this catalogue

Author data is shown exactly as it appears in the catalogue record. No record is linked to a project yet, so the project filter currently returns nothing when set; links appear once the records carry a project field.

The records are harvested from Zenodo. Follow a record to its page for the full keyword list and the DOI, or go to the research projects to see what each SSP works on.
