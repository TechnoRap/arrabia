Research

Eight projects, one climate system

Consequences of climate change are emerging in the oceans, ice, land and atmosphere. EMBRACER studies the feedbacks between them: processes whose impacts are expected to stay relatively minor over the coming years to decades, yet will determine Earth's climate far beyond 2100.

Ocean Land & ice Life Water

Themes

The programme connects different Earth-system components, timescales and methods. Earth scientists, geochemists, oceanographers, polar researchers, hydrologists and ecologists collaborate to bridge the knowledge gap between predicting short-term and long-term climate change.

Each project is a Sub-Study Project (SSP). Together they cover ocean carbon, ice sheets and sea level, vegetation and fire, and the water cycle from mountains to the sea, on timescales of decades to millennia.
